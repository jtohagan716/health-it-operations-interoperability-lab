FHIR R4 MedicationRequest dispense quantity verification

Specification:
HL7 FHIR R4 4.0.1

Element:
MedicationRequest.dispenseRequest.quantity

Definition:
The amount that is to be dispensed for one fill.

Interpretation:
dispenseRequest.quantity represents the amount supplied for a fill.
It is semantically distinct from the medication administration dose represented
by dosageInstruction.doseAndRate.doseQuantity.

The FHIR specification describes MedicationRequest as containing both medication
supply information and administration instructions.
